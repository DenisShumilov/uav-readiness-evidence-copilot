Engineering projects often contain many scattered files: a parts list, a manual, test logs, and QA notes.
Before a review, the team needs one clear answer: what is actually supported by evidence?

This is UAV Readiness & Evidence Copilot.
It reads safe synthetic demo files and turns them into a documentation readiness package.

The demo page shows a documentation readiness score.
It also shows three counters: verified, partial, and locked.
Verified means the evidence is present.
Partial means the evidence is incomplete.
Locked means the tool will not approve a claim when proof is missing.

Next, the page highlights warnings and locked items.
This is the core behavior: the workflow does not invent confidence.

After the review, the tool generates useful outputs: a report, an evidence graph, a traceability table, and file hashes.
That gives the team a clearer handoff package for engineering review.

This is not a drone-control system.
It is a safe documentation QA workflow for engineering review.
