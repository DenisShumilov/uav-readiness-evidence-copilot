Engineering projects often have scattered files: parts lists, notes, review logs, and claims.
Before a handoff, the simple question is: what is actually backed by evidence?

This is UAV Readiness & Evidence Copilot.
This is not drone control.
This is QA and documentation readiness.
It does not use live aircraft data, routes, or operational systems.

The demo shows a readiness score, evidence counters, warnings, and locked items.
Readiness score means documentation readiness, not permission to operate equipment.
Verified means there is proof.
Partial means the proof is incomplete.
Locked means the tool refuses to approve an item when evidence is missing.

Behind the page, a TypeScript CLI reads synthetic demo files.
It builds an evidence graph, which is a map of claims and proof.
Then it creates a report, traceability CSV, artifact hashes, and portfolio pages.

The value is safe engineering QA: evidence tracking, traceability, and readiness reporting.
For a recruiter, this shows practical internal-tool thinking, clear safety boundaries, and testable TypeScript.
