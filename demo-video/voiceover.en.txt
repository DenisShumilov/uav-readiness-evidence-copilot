UAV Readiness and Evidence Copilot is a safe documentation QA demo. It does not control a drone. It checks whether the paperwork can support its own claims.

This live readiness check starts at forty-four out of a hundred. The status is derived from evidence. Turn off the BOM source, then the manual source: connected claims become locked, and the gauge falls from forty-four to twenty-six. No evidence means locked. The tool does not invent support. Reset brings the evidence back.

The evidence graph is driven by that same toggle state. When a source is turned off, its edges and dependent claims turn locked red. Turn it back on, and the derived statuses recover.

Here is the conflict gate. Two synthetic documents are each hand-marked verified, but they disagree. The derived verdict stays Blocked at forty-nine out of a hundred, even though every row was marked verified.
