# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of experience
level, background, or identity. As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make participation a
respectful experience for all.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- being kind, patient, and welcoming, especially to newcomers;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its learning goals;
- respecting the project's strict safety scope (documentation QA only).

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments;
- publishing others' private information without permission;
- requests to add operational UAV capability (flight control, routing, telemetry, targeting,
  payload, tactics) — these are out of scope and will be declined;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when
an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer via the
contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
