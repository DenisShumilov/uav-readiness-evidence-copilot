# Code of Conduct

*[Українською](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.uk.md) · English*

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity. As contributors and maintainers, we commit to making participation a respectful experience for all.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community:

- being kind, patient, and welcoming, especially to newcomers;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its educational goals;
- respecting the strict safety scope of the project (documentation QA only).

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments;
- publishing others' private information without permission;
- requests to add operational UAV capabilities (flight control, routing, live telemetry control, targeting, payload operation, tactics) — these are outside the project's safety boundary and will be declined;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer via the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
